Once you have your customers in your CRM, you are ready to create template messages to broadcast to them. It's required by Meta that you can only initiate the conversation with your customer through template messages. Create your first template message in Assets -> Templates page.

Our template creation system is not fully featured compared to WhatsApp Manager. In particular, we do not provide Flow buttons which requires many other configurations. Flow buttons allow your customers to fill up a form and receive events when user completes a form. For most of other types of templates, such as image, video, document, location, quick reply button, visit website, copy offer code, call phone number are all available.

Every new template created has to go through Meta review. You will get notified when a template is approved or rejected (with reason). Any subsequent changes to an already approved template has to go through Meta review again. There is no limit on number of templates you can create.
