It's time to add customers to your CRM. You can do it in the Chats page, where everything related to your customers happen. Use the Add customer button to either create a customer manually or import using .vcf file (exportable from your iPhone or Android phone).

Each customer has to be assigned to a phone number. Any incoming messages from a unsaved customer will create a new customer in the database. If an existing customer messages another phone number in your business account, a new customer record will be created.
