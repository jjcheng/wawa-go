Now that you have your customers and template messages ready, you can start a broadcast campaign in the Chats page by selecting at least 1 customer you wish to send broadcast to. In the create broadcast page, enter the broadcast name, select send now or later (no limit on how late it will be), enter variable values like customer name, see the preview of the template, and Send.

You will get notified when the broadcast started. Once it starts, you can go to Broadcasts page to view the status of the broadcast campaign. You can go a step deeper, to see which messages are sent, delivered, read or rejected. If an error occurred, we will try to send again for another 2 times. In the event that the message cannot be sent after 3 tries, a notification will be sent to you as well.
